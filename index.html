About

I’m a Lead Product Designer with 10+ years designing complex SaaS products, scalable design systems, and AI-native experiences. My work sits at the intersection of product strategy, systems thinking, and execution — I own ambiguous problems and build frameworks that scale beyond a single feature or team.

At Constant Contact, I lead design across core product, reporting, and design systems — owning the AI assistant experience, defining component standards in Summit (the company’s unified design system), and shaping how AI-powered insights reach small business marketers at scale. Before that, at Ninety.io, I launched a mobile app from 0→1 that reached Apple’s Top 100 Business Apps, co-built Terra (the company’s first design system), and shipped an AI agent with a 58% task completion rate.

I think in systems, not just screens. Whether I’m defining an AI interaction model, establishing governance for a component library, or redesigning a complex multi-step workflow, I care about the craft: interaction design, information architecture, accessibility, and visual quality. I use Claude, Cursor, ChatGPT, and Gemini as daily parts of my process — for research synthesis, prototype generation, documentation, and implementation.

Outside work, I photograph families and newborns, experiment with new AI tools, and spend time with my husband and two daughters in Upstate New York.
